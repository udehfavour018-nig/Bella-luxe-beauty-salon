## Bug report

**Describe the bug**
The mobile navigation button is not visible or is difficult to use on smaller screens, making it hard to open the menu.

**Steps to reproduce**
1. Open the site on a phone or narrow browser window
2. Observe the navigation area
3. Try to open the menu

**Expected behavior**
A clear menu icon should always appear on mobile and open the nav items reliably.

**Additional context**
This is especially important for users visiting from smartphones.
